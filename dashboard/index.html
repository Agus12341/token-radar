Token Launch Radar

Every protocol that still records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth — and how to farm it.

How the radar values a launch

implied value = TVL × category multiple × momentum × seasoning

Category multiple

The median market-cap / TVL ratio of protocols in the same DefiLlama category whose token already trades, blended toward the global median () when a category has few traded tokens, and clamped between 0.03 and 3. A lending market is not priced like a DEX, because the snapshot's own traded tokens say so.

Momentum & seasoning

Momentum is the protocol's 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±30% and worth at most ±15%. Seasoning is how long it has run with no token recorded, on a five-year square-root curve worth 0.95× to 1.30×. Nothing on the board is keyed to a name: rerun the program on a fresher snapshot and the board rebuilds itself.

Who is on the board

Protocols that record no token (no CoinGecko id, no symbol) and are not a wrapped, staked, bridged or versioned product of one that trades; protocols whose token already trades are set aside, not ranked. The house watchlist is covered in full — and the radar keeps going below its floor, where launches are earliest.
